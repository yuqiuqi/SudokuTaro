## 01 · 新叶 / New Leaves

主线完成后的第1次新胜局 · New win 1 after the main story

### 中文

薄荷冒出两片新叶。我拍给姐姐看，她回了一张工作室的花盆。我问能不能剪一点泡水，她画了条短线，告诉我从哪里下剪。剪下的小枝留在盆边，闻起来很清凉。

### English

Two new leaves appeared on the mint. I sent my sister a photograph, and she sent one of the pot in her studio. I asked whether I could cut a little for tea. She drew a short line on my photograph to show me where. The sprig I cut lay beside the pot, smelling cool and fresh.

## 02 · 先留一杯 / A Cup for Later

主线完成后的第2次新胜局 · New win 2 after the main story

### 中文

我把薄荷放进小杯，问姐姐要不要尝。她说还在工作室收版，要晚一点。我没继续添热水，把杯子搁在盆旁。等她到了，再一起泡；这会儿，我先喝自己的。

### English

I put the mint in a little cup and asked my sister if she wanted some. She was still putting away printing plates at her studio and would be a while. I left the cup beside the pot without adding hot water. We could brew it when she arrived. For now, I drank my own tea.

## 03 · 把手旁边 / Beside the Handle

主线完成后的第3次新胜局 · New win 3 after the main story

### 中文

擦窗布总被我收得找不着。姐姐说，工作室把常用的布挂在伸手够得到的地方。我在铜把手旁添了一个小钩。擦完玻璃，布还潮着，我把它摊开挂好，没急着藏起来。

### English

I kept putting the window cloth away so carefully that I could never find it. My sister said they hung the cloths they used most within easy reach at the studio. I added a little hook beside the brass handle. After cleaning the glass, I spread the damp cloth out and hung it up. It did not need to be hidden away.

## 04 · 合上的画册 / The Closed Sketchbook

主线完成后的第4次新胜局 · New win 4 after the main story

### 中文

姐姐带来一本画册，翻到一半，接到工作室的电话。她问能不能先放窗台，下回接着看。我合上书，把干净的窗帘挪开一点。窗台不大，放一本书正好。

### English

My sister brought a sketchbook. Halfway through showing it to me, she took a call from the studio. Could she leave it on the windowsill so we could look at the rest another time? I closed it and moved the clean curtain aside a little. The sill was small, but there was room for one book.

## 05 · 垫子朝哪面 / Which Side of the Cushion

主线完成后的第5次新胜局 · New win 5 after the main story

### 中文

我用余下的窗帘布缝了椅垫，两面颜色不同。姐姐坐下前翻了一面，说这样看得清布纹。我也翻了翻自己那只。两把椅子各有各的颜色，没有必要摆成一套。

### English

I made cushions from the leftover curtain fabric, with a different color on each side. Before sitting down, my sister turned hers over. She could see the weave better that way, she said. I tried turning mine over, too. Each chair had its own color. They did not have to match.

## 06 · 杯子挪近一点 / A Little Closer

主线完成后的第6次新胜局 · New win 6 after the main story

### 中文

姐姐坐在窗边，把桌上第二只杯子往椅子旁挪了挪。我差点说那里不整齐，话到嘴边停住了。她一伸手就能拿到，我也把自己的杯子拉近一点。茶还温着。

### English

Sitting by the window, my sister moved the second cup on the table closer to her chair. I almost told her it looked untidy there, then stopped. She could reach it easily. I pulled my own cup a little closer, too. The tea was still warm.

## 07 · 一小块蓝布 / A Small Blue Patch

主线完成后的第7次新胜局 · New win 7 after the main story

### 中文

桌布又磨薄了一角。我先问陶姨愿不愿意教我，她说喝完茶再看。我们挑了一小块蓝布，接在原来的绿线旁。补片没遮住旧针脚，两种颜色都留了下来。

### English

Another corner of the tablecloth had worn thin. I asked Auntie Tao whether she felt like showing me how to mend it. We could look after tea, she said. We chose a small piece of blue fabric and sewed it beside the old green stitches. The patch did not cover them. Both colors stayed.

## 08 · 盖好针线盒 / Closing the Sewing Box

主线完成后的第8次新胜局 · New win 8 after the main story

### 中文

补完最后一针，我还想把椅套也拿来。陶姨看了看表，说该去排练了。我把针插回针垫，盖好盒子。她走前摸了摸新补片，说下回先看看我自己缝成什么样。

### English

After the last stitch, I wanted to fetch a chair cover as well. Auntie Tao checked the time. She needed to leave for rehearsal. I returned the needle to its cushion and closed the box. Before going, she ran a hand over the new patch. Next time, she said, she would like to see what I had managed to sew on my own.

## 09 · 纸签留白 / Room on the Paper Tag

主线完成后的第9次新胜局 · New win 9 after the main story

### 中文

姐姐从工作室带来一条窄纸，挂在风铃下面。我问要写什么，她说先空着也行。风吹过，纸签翻了个面，只露出背后试印的一点浅绿。我们都没有再添字。

### English

My sister brought a narrow strip of paper from her studio and hung it beneath the wind chime. I asked what we should write on it. We could leave it blank for now, she said. A breeze turned it over, revealing a little pale green from a test print on the back. Neither of us added any words.

## 10 · 收起的伞 / The Folded Umbrella

主线完成后的第10次新胜局 · New win 10 after the main story

### 中文

陶姨来还上回借的伞，进门前在檐下把水抖干。她只坐了一会儿，还要去见合唱团的朋友。我把伞收好靠在墙边，杯底的一圈水慢慢干了，风铃仍轻轻地响。

### English

Auntie Tao returned the umbrella she had borrowed, shaking off the water under the eaves before coming in. She stayed only a little while; she was meeting friends from her choir. I folded the umbrella and leaned it against the wall. The ring of water beneath her cup slowly dried. The wind chime made its small sound.

## 11 · 纸牌上的夹子 / A Clip on the Door Note

主线完成后的第11次新胜局 · New win 11 after the main story

### 中文

门边的纸牌被风掀起，姐姐用工作室带来的夹子夹住一角。我想再写满几条约定，她指了指那句“来前说一声”。我们只把卷边压平，给旁边留了一块空白。

### English

The wind lifted the note beside the door. My sister secured one corner with a clip from her studio. I wanted to fill the remaining space with a few more arrangements. She pointed to “Let us know you are coming.” We smoothed the curled edge and left some room beside it.

## 12 · 三只不同的杯子 / Three Different Cups

主线完成后的第12次新胜局 · New win 12 after the main story

### 中文

陶姨带了点心，姐姐从工作室过来，桌上摆了三只不同的杯子。吃完各自收拾，没有谁留下来守院子。我把这些小事记到这里，合上册子；灯留着，自己的日子也还在继续。

### English

Auntie Tao brought a few snacks, and my sister came over from her studio. Three different cups stood on the table. Afterwards, we each helped clear up. No one stayed behind to keep watch over the courtyard. This was where I ended my notes on these small things and closed the booklet. I left the light on. There was still my own life to get on with.
